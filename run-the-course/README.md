# Run the Course

A Bible running game for children aged 6–12, in the style of Temple Run or Subway Surfers.
It is built on 2 Timothy 4:7, Hebrews 12:1 and Philippians 3:13: a race with a real prize,
run in the strength of the Lord.

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. There is no build step and nothing to install.
It loads two Google Fonts (Lilita One and Fredoka) and works with any static web host.

## What happens in the game

- **Prayer first.** Each race starts with the runner kneeling and the child tapping "Amen".
- **Move.** Swipe, or use the arrow keys: left and right change lanes, up jumps, down slides.
- **Temptations** (Malice, Lying, Envy, Greed and others) are pink signs. Running into one
  stops the runner while the clock keeps going. The child taps "I'm sorry, Lord" and a beam
  of light lifts the runner up again (1 John 1:9).
- **Weights** (hurdles, beams, stones) make the runner stumble (Hebrews 12:1).
- **The Fruit of the Spirit** is already planted in the runner's heart and grows from seed to
  ripe fruit as the child flees temptation, does good works and collects living water.
- **Good works** are green signs: give to the poor, lay hands on the sick, serve at church,
  comfort a friend, share the Gospel, carry a burden. The clock waits while the child serves,
  and each good work adds a jewel to the crown.
- **The finish** ends with laying the crown at Jesus' feet (Revelation 4:10–11).

There are seven courses, each with its own sky, plus the endless **Marathon of Faith**,
which unlocks after the first course.

Kids choose their runner (skin, hair, jersey, number, headband) and modest clothing:
track pants, knee-length shorts, or a skirt with leggings.

## Privacy

Nothing is sent anywhere. Progress, stars and the chosen runner are kept only in the
player's own browser (`localStorage`). There is no visit counting inside the game.

All Bible quotations are from the King James Version.
