Run theCourse

The crown is waiting at the finish line. Run in the strength of the Lord: jump the hurdles, flee every temptation, and stop to help people in need along the way.

  • 2 Tim. 4:7“I have finished my course, I have kept the faith.”
  • Heb. 12:1“Let us lay aside every weight… and let us run with patience the race that is set before us.”
  • Phil. 3:13“Forgetting those things which are behind… I press toward the mark.”
  • 1 Cor. 15:10“By the grace of God I am what I am.”

How to run

🏃

Move

Swipe, or use the arrow keys. ←→ switch lanes, ↑ jump, ↓ slide.

😈

Flee temptation

Pink signs like Malice, Lying and Envy try to pull you in. Touch one and your runner stops to do it while the clock ticks. Say “I’m sorry, Lord” and His grace lifts you up.

🪨

Lay aside the weights

Hurdles, beams and stones make you stumble. Grab 🛡️ Shield of Faith, 🦅 Eagle Wings and ⏳ extra time.

🌱

Grow the fruit in your heart

All 9 Fruit of the Spirit are already planted in you. Flee temptation, do good works and drink 💧 living water, and they grow until they are ripe.

🙏

Do good works

Green signs show people who need help. Steer to them to feed the hungry, pray for the sick or serve at church. The clock waits while you serve.

👑

Win the prize

Pray, then run. Reach the finish before time runs out and lay your crown at Jesus’ feet. Stars: finish, flee every temptation, grow all 9 fruit.
